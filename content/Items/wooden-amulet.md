These amulets were created by [Luminaire Foscari](../NPCs/luminaire-foscari) and [Dr. Collodi](../NPCs/dr-collodi), presumably from the world tree sapling; by resonating with the innate mana field of the person wearing them, they create a single-person-sized shield against the miasma that allows individuals to venture into the miasma without fear.
