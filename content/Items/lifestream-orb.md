This gently glowing green magical orb originated at the top of the Crawling Tower. It used to exude a sense of revulsion when touched, but that lessened over time as [Cynthia](cynthia-schreiber.md) got used to it. 

Its first ability has been unlocked: gazing into it allows one to see the flow of soul energy. Living beings often appear as bright green silhouettes overlapping their body; miasma appears as a sludgy green fog, revealing that it is made up of souls that have not returned to the Lifestream. Interestingly, [Serafina](serafina.md) appears as bright green as any other living person, but her soul energy is tightly packed into a spot inside her chest. Wonder what that's all about?
