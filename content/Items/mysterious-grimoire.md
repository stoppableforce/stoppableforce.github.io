Currently owned by [Irving Glenn](../Players/irving-glenn), the mysterious grimoire was found in a part of the Lycaeum library that contained tomes that were forbidden or otherwise off-limits. Desperate for a lead in his research on the miasma, Irving claimed the grimoire as his own -- and has found it knows things a book has no sense knowing.

## Known Properties of the Grimoire

- It always has blank pages at the back. 
- The pages at the front contain a wealth of knowledge, including prior questions asked, but if you cannot find an answer, you can flip to a blank page and write your question. The Grimoire will respond on its own by writing back in turn.
- It has claimed that its prior owner was Genevieve Gallo, but Irving doesn't know that name.
- It predates the miasmic age.
