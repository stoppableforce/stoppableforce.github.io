The Clockwork City of Orologio is the city [Serafina](../Player%20Characters/serafina) is from. It's something of a haven for tinkerers and magitechnicians, and is ruled by a body of the most capable crafters, the Mechatriarchy.

It was pictured on the map in the [library's map room](../Places/library-under-mountain), with a small knocked-over playing piece next to it. Something may have happened to cause this recently -- the letter that Serafina was carrying, from [Galeran Avelot](../NPCs/galeran-avelot), stated that the Mechatriarchy believed they had a solution to the miasma situation, but that Galeran felt it was too risky.
