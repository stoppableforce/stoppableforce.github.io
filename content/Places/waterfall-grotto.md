Located slightly northeast of [Bihara](bihara) and accessible from the sea, the waters of the Waterfall Grotto are a place where the Lifestream surfaces and freely flows, mingling with regular water. While inside the Waterfall Grotto, the party can perform rituals of the Ritualism and Spiritualism disciplines to access ancient memories and visions.
