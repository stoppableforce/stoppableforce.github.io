Not much is known in-character yet about Lehrenstein.

* It has stratified class divsions - the upper class often have magically-enhanced houses, such as magically-supported spiraling ramps. (The ones in the Crawling Tower reminded Cynthia of home.)
* The Schreiber estate is a great manor house behind a gated wall, separated in multiple buildings behind a gate that is normally open. An illusion of it was seen while looking for the [Metamorphic Horn](Metamorphic%20Horn.md) with a locked gate.
