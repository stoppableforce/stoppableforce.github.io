Marilun was once a prosperous mining town, but that was long ago. Now the mines have dried up, and the miasma makes it difficult to try and find new veins in the mountain. However, a new source of interest has perhaps put the town back on a few people's radar; while in the old mines refilling the water for their world tree sapling, a party of locals accidentally uncovered [a very old, very large library](../Places/library-under-mountain).
