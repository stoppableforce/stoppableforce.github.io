The river-straddling fishing town of Ostro is the far eastern edge of what is considered the Bluewick region of the world. This town lies on both the east and west sides of the Lucent River, and the primary activity in town is fishing -- both river fishing locally, and sea fishing up the Lucent -- and trade, even in these beset times. Ostro is something of a midway point between [Bluewick](bluewick) and [Marilun](marilun) to the west, and [Nuono](nuono), [Bihara](bihara), and [Orologio](orologio) to the east; the town and even the bridge itself are dotted with traders' stalls, even if they don't see as much traffic as they did years ago.
