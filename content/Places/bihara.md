Bihara has been destroyed; its world tree sapling is burnt and hollow, drained of life. The surrounding area is devoid even of color; oddly, it is also completely devoid of miasma.

The streets of the town are seemingly roamed by anti-life tentacles, made of strange void energy, controlled by a beast dubbed the "phantom hunger" that perches atop the building that used to house the sapling.

Survivors seem to have escaped to [Nuono](nuono) to the northeast.
