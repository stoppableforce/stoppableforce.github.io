Our party's home base. Bluewick is really two distinct places settled by the side of a river: the Lycaeum, one of the world's foremost places of learning (even prior to the Age of Miasma), and the town of Bluewick that grew up around it -- initially to cater to the needs and wants of an educational institution, and eventually as a town in its own right.
