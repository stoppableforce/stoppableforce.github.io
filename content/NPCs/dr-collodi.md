Pronouns: she/her
Met: while rescuing her from the Library Under the Mountain
Role: Magitechnickal researcher at the Lycaeum

Dr. Collodi is one of the few researchers at the Lycaeum who deals with both magic and technology, making her an ideal collaborator with [Professor Foscari](luminaire-foscari) on the [wooden amulets](../Items/wooden-amulet) to allow individuals to venture into the miasma unaided by a water vessel.

However, while on a trip to [Marilun](../Places/marilun) to perform a field test of the amulets with a couple of research assistants, Collodi was seemingly sidetracked to the [library under the mountain](../Places/library-under-mountain) -- and has been missing for a week or two, enough that Professor Foscari was worried and sent the party to check on her.
