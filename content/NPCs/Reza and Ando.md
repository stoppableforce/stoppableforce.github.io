Pronouns: he/him (for both)
Met: in Nuono, after they fled Bihara
Role: Alchemists

Before the town was overrun, Reza and Ando operated the alchemist shop that produced the mermaid potions the party used to reach Nuono -- and the same potions several Biharans _also_ used to reach Nuono. They were two of the earliest to step up to help take their town back.
