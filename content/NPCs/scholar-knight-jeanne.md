A mysterious knight met in the [Library Under the Mountain](../Places/library-under-mountain). Seemingly had held [Dr. Collodi](dr-collodi) and her work party hostage simply due to them stumbling across her work -- the purpose of which was not entirely clear.

Was primarily seen wielding dark magic in combat before beating a hasty retreat when outmatched.

The party came upon Jeanne later [on the way to Ostro](../Adventure Log/session_5). Jeanne mentioned that she is part of a group called the Black Seal Knights, and confirmed that Irving and the grimoire had already deduced -- the game pieces on the map found in the library corresponded to her comrades. She also filled the group in somewhat on the situation in [Orologio](/places/orologio), noting that their solution to the miasma -- going back in time to before the event that caused it, and stopping it -- had instead simply had the unfortunate side effect of "unsticking" Orologio in time.
