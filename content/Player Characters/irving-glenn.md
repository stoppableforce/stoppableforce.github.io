## Background
Irving was once a scholar of little repute, until he entered a locked-off part of the library in his never-ending quest to find the source of the miasma and rid the world of it, taking with him a mysterious tome. This ignoble act has cost Irving much of his standing at the Lycaeum -- but if anything else, it has only increased his desire to rid the world of the poison that threatens to choke it.
## Fun Facts
- Irving mostly does "grunt TA work" now.
- Irving talks to the grimoire by writing his question on a blank page, and it writes back. The entire group has now seen this happen.
