## Background
Ysildea is the knight captain of the small standing army of Nuono, and is the right hand of Queen Veris III. She was first encountered as part of the test to see if the party was willing to contribute to the city's well-being in the face of the refugee influx, and later accompanied them to retrieve the [Metamorphic Horn](Metamorphic%20Horn.md).
## Fun Facts
* Ysildea has only barely begun actually learning the party's names. She still tends to refer to Cynthia as "sorceress," Irving as "scholar," and Serafina as "fisher girl."
