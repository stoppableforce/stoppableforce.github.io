## Background
Serafina was created at some point in [Orologio](../Places/orologio) by [Galeran Avelot](../NPCs/galeran-avelot), as a "solution to the miasma problem." When the Mechatriarchy of Orologio decided to pursue their own solution that Galeran deemed too dangerous, he sent Serafina -- along with a letter explaining this in not-detailed terms -- to Dr. Collodi. 

Serafina came wandering in from out of the miasma one day, mysteriously unharmed; while she was mistrusted by the residents of Bluewick at first, she is now generally accepted -- if nothing else, for her willingness to pitch in and do her part for the town.

## Fun Facts
- When she came out of the miasma, Serafina had a letter to [Dr. Collodi](../NPCs/dr-collodi) from [Galeran Avelot](../NPCs/galeran-avelot). She didn't read it at any point before delivering it.
- Serafina lives at the inn. She likes to cook, but she isn't very good at it.
  - This doesn't stop Mero from eating it.
  - While in Nuono, however, she learned how to make sushi by trial and error.
- Serafina has a strong negative (sad) reaction to Orologio. This is unusual, as she doesn't usually have strong emotional reactions to much of *anything*.
- Serafina can either understand what [Mero](../NPCs/mero) is saying, or can guess it really well.
- Serafina sinks to the bottom of bodies of water naturally, but can choose to float.
- When viewed through the [Lifestream Orb](../Items/lifestream-orb), Serafina's soul energy appears tightly compacted in a single spot in the middle of her chest, rather than as a silhouette of her whole body like a human's.
